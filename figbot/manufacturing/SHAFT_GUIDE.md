# ARM-007 joint-shaft guide

Status: `UNVERIFIED — PHYSICAL VALIDATION REQUIRED`

ARM-007 denotes the joint shaft family. Distinct lengths or features must receive
unique drawing/configuration identifiers before release; never rely on operator
memory to select a shaft.

## Turning sequence

1. Confirm released material, stock diameter, heat treatment and revision. These are
   not fixed by this guide.
2. Face and center; rough-turn leaving a documented finishing allowance.
3. Finish bearing and seal seats in a controlled setup. Add shoulders, grooves,
   threads or drive flats only where shown on the drawing.
4. Deburr without rounding functional shoulders. Clean abrasives/chips completely.
5. Identify the part without marking a bearing surface or fatigue-critical section.

## Inspection

Measure overall/shoulder lengths, bearing-seat diameters, runout, shoulder squareness,
surface condition and thread/retainer features using instruments appropriate to the
released tolerance. The controlled material candidates are C45 or 42CrMo4+QT.
ARM-007 is 15 mm nominal and mates to 6002-2RS; ARM-008 is 8 mm nominal and
mates to 608-2RS. Proposed g6 journals and H7 housing limits are recorded in
`INTERFACE_CONTROL.csv`. Retaining grooves, shoulder stack, surface roughness
and final drawing approval remain `BLOCKED_FOR_DRAWING_SIGNOFF`.

Before assembly, verify the shaft slides/presses only by the approved method. Never
drive force through bearing rolling elements. Rotational freedom and axial retention
must be checked after installation. Calculations or fit checks do not establish
fatigue life or safe load capacity.

## Material selection gate

Select only silicone with supplier documentation for the intended food-contact
jurisdiction and curing process. Shore hardness, tear strength, mix ratio, cure,
post-cure, pigment, release agent and cleaning chemistry are `TBD`. Supplier claims
do not validate the finished molded part or workshop cleanliness.

## Controlled trial procedure

1. Inspect/clean mold and record GRP-005/006/007 revision, print lot and cavity state.
2. Verify silicone components, lot, shelf life and supplier instructions. Wear the
   SDS-specified PPE and provide required ventilation.
3. Apply only a reviewed compatible release system; prevent residue on final product
   contact surfaces or document the cleaning validation.
4. Weigh components using the supplier ratio. Record actual masses, room/material
   temperature, start time and operator.
5. Mix and degas as supplier directs. Fill from the lowest point to reduce trapped
   air. Retain a witness coupon from the same batch.
6. Cure and post-cure strictly within supplier conditions; do not accelerate without
   approval. Demold without cutting functional contact surfaces.
7. Inspect bubbles, tears, sticky/uncured areas, contamination, flashing and geometry.
   Clean with the approved method and label lot/variant.

Hardness must be measured on a suitable witness specimen, not inferred from the
product geometry. Release to fruit trials only after the GRP damage-test plan is
approved. Never claim the fingers do not damage figs without physical results.

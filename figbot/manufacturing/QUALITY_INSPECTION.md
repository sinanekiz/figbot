# V0 manufacturing inspection and traceability

Status: `UNVERIFIED — HUMAN MANUFACTURING REVIEW REQUIRED`

Every custom part traveler records part number/revision, quantity, source file hash,
material/lot, process/equipment, operator, date, inspection instrument/asset ID,
measured characteristics, nonconformance disposition and final reviewer.

First-article inspection is required after a design revision, material/process change,
tooling repair or supplier change. At minimum verify identity, count, obvious defects,
drawing-critical dimensions, mating interfaces, cable/product-contact hazards and
cleanliness. Critical dimensions and sampling levels come from released drawings;
this guide does not invent tolerances.

Nonconforming material is segregated and labeled. Rework requires written instructions
and reinspection; “fits after forcing” is not acceptance. Measurement results must be
retained with the prototype serial/configuration so physical tests are reproducible.

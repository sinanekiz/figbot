# V0 inspection, maintenance and recalibration

Status: `DRAFT — INTERVALS TBD — PHYSICAL VALIDATION REQUIRED`

Before each test session, inspect anchoring, printed housings, shafts/retainers, coupling
tracking, gripper fingers, funnel/liner, camera rigidity, cables/connectors, guards and
E-stop hardware. Compare fastener witness marks and check calibration points with
drives disabled. Any crack, movement, contamination, abnormal play/noise or residual
shift blocks powered testing.

After any impact/collision, transport, camera/mount adjustment, joint disassembly,
software frame change or unexplained pick-position shift, invalidate calibration and
repeat the camera-to-robot calibration procedure. Replace damaged silicone contacts;
record new part revision, lot and use count.

Service intervals cannot be inferred before endurance testing. Define them from T08
wear/drift evidence with conservative human review. Lock out and verify absence of
energy before mechanical/electrical maintenance. Cleaning methods for product-contact
parts require food-contact/process review and material compatibility evidence.
